# Module Registry and Roadmap

The advisory instrument is six **modules** that assemble into one coverage position, three
**worksheets** that do one job each, and eighteen more tools specified but not built.

Machine-readable: `src/content/modules/` and `src/content/tools/`. This file explains the
decisions behind them, in particular how eligibility, carrier appetite, and pricing are
handled.

Last updated: 2026-09-02

## Modules versus worksheets

The distinction is real, not cosmetic.

| | Module | Worksheet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it assesses | A line, as a standing fact about your coverage | One document in front of you now |
| Where it writes | The shared coverage position | Nowhere. Local to the page. |
| Cross-checks | Yes. Its facts raise items in other modules. | No. |
| Counted in the position | Yes | No |
| Examples | Property, earthquake, workers compensation | Compare two quotes, map one contract |

A specific pair of quotes is not a standing fact about your coverage, which is why the policy
comparison worksheet does not write into the position. A roof age is, which is why the property
module does.

## The hard problem: eligibility, appetite, and pricing

The specialty domains are advertised on the three things a public insurance page most easily
gets wrong. Each is resolved the same way throughout: **publish the evidence, never the
verdict.**

| The ask | What a naive build would do | What this instrument does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Gauge eligibility" | Output "you qualify for the standard market" | **Placement pathways.** Enumerate the market channels that exist in published law or a published program, each with its cited entry criteria, its effective date, the documentation the program itself asks for, and the body that decides. A map of routes, never a routing decision. |
| "Carrier pricing and appetites" | Summarize which carriers write what | **Appetite evidence.** An appetite statement may appear only as a quotation from, or a direct link to, the carrier's own published document, labelled carrier-authored and dated. This site never writes its own summary of a carrier's appetite and never infers it from placement experience. |
| "Pricing estimations" | Produce an estimated premium | **Filed rate evidence.** Only figures a public authority has actually published: WCIRB advisory pure premium rates, NCCI loss cost filings, state premium comparison tools, FEMA NFIP methodology. Each carries its publisher, filing date, effective date, and link, plus an explicit list of what it excludes. The tool never multiplies published components together to produce an implied premium. |

The reasoning is not squeamishness. An eligibility verdict on a public page is wrong a
meaningful share of the time, because underwriting turns on facts the page does not have, and
being wrong either costs someone coverage they could have had or promises coverage they cannot
get. An advisory pure premium rate labelled as a price is wrong every time, because it excludes
experience modification, schedule rating, carrier deviation, expense load, minimum premiums,
and assessments.

The evidence version is also the better product. Someone who can see the published FAIR Plan
entry criteria, the surplus lines diligent-search requirement, and a carrier's own appetite
guide, each dated and linked, is better equipped than someone handed a verdict they cannot
check.

## What a rule may be

Five kinds, and nothing else. This is enforced by `validateModule()` in `src/lib/position.ts`
and asserted by `scripts/verify-instrument.mjs`, so a rule that crosses the line fails the
build.

| Kind | What it does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **gap** | A described activity with no matching coverage recorded | Design services recorded, general liability recorded, professional liability not |
| **inconsistency** | Arithmetic or exact comparison on two recorded values | A contract requires a $2M aggregate; $1M is recorded |
| **timing** | Arithmetic on recorded dates against a cited deadline | A restore test older than the cadence the cited framework states |
| **documentation** | An item a cited source says is required or commonly requested, marked as not held | A dwelling limit recorded with no written replacement cost estimate |
| **question** | A question worth asking, generated by what was recorded, naming who to ask | Knob and tube wiring recorded |

Every rule is deterministic: exact comparison or arithmetic on recorded fields. No scoring, no
weighting, no probability. A rule needing judgement is not a rule; it becomes a question the
module asks.

Two further properties the test suite enforces:

- **No rule fires on an empty position.** Someone who has recorded nothing sees nothing. An
  open item a person cannot act on is worse than none.
- **Every rule is reachable.** Some recorded state must make it fire. A rule that can never
  fire is dead weight that looks like coverage.

Each module page now also carries the worked examples on its line, so a reader arriving from a

not built: no member health information, no individual census rows, ever.

## Live worksheets

| Worksheet | Route | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Insurance Requirement Mapper | `/tools/requirement-mapper` | Turns one agreement's insurance clause into requested / confirmed / open question, per row. Two independent states that are never merged. |
| Renewal Readiness Checklist | `/tools/renewal-readiness` | Documents, schedules, and change triggers before a renewal, filtered to the lines in force. |
| Policy Comparison Workspace | `/tools/policy-comparison` | Two or three policies across the sixteen terms that actually change what you are protected against. Generates the question list. |

`contract-requirements` (module) and `requirement-mapper` (worksheet) look adjacent and are
not. The module records limits and endorsements as standing facts so other modules can check
against them. The worksheet organizes one specific contract clause by clause. Both pages say so.

## Specified, not built

Eighteen entries in `src/content/tools/`. None has a route, a sitemap entry, or a navigation
link, because an empty tool page is worse than no tool page, and `scripts/verify.mjs` asserts
it.

Each names its user, input data, authoritative sources, privacy boundary, decision rules,
uncertainty, output, export format, accessibility states, schema eligibility, review owner, and
update cadence. A tool that cannot answer all twelve is not specified well enough to build.

### The comprehensiveness gap, scoped

The dimensions are now in place on every published line: exposures, claim mitigation,
underwriting requirements, limits and conditions, endorsements, state variations, all sourced.
What is not in place is breadth. Six coverage pages is not "all types of insurance", and the
domain inventory says exactly which lines are missing rather than leaving it to judgement.

**Published lines (6):** general liability, commercial property, homeowners, dwelling fire,
residential earthquake, workers compensation.

**Lines a held domain names and no page answers (10 domains, 8 distinct lines):**

| Line | Domains waiting | What the page needs before it can publish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Directors and officers | `bestdando.com`, `bestdirectorsandofficers.com` | A D&O form to read. Side A/B/C structure, the insured-vs-insured exclusion, and entity coverage are the substance. |
| Commercial auto | `bestcommercialauto.com` | The business auto coverage form, symbol structure, and hired and non-owned. The contract module already records auto limits without explaining them. |
| Personal auto | `bestpersonalauto.com` | A personal auto form plus the state minimums already sourced on the state pages. |
| Products and completed operations | `bestproductliability.com` | The CGL page names the separate aggregate; the line needs its own page for recall, vendors endorsements, and the hazard definition. |
| Umbrella and excess | `bestexcess.com` | A following-form umbrella and the underlying-limit requirement the contract module already references. |
| Surplus lines | `bestsurpluslines.com` | The diligent-search requirement and the stamping fee. Overlaps the `placement-pathways` tool on the roadmap. |
| Builders risk | `bestbuildersrisk.com` | A builders risk form. Named in the inland marine question, covered nowhere. |
| HOA and common interest | `besthoainsurance.com` | Master policy versus unit owner allocation. The earthquake module records master-policy status without explaining it. |

**Lines that have a question but not a coverage page (5 domains):** professional liability and
E&O, inland marine, surety, replacement cost, difference in conditions. Each currently lands on a
question or an example. That is honest and useful, and a line page would be better.

**Industry pages (13 domains).** A different kind of asset from a line page: same lines, organised
by who is buying. None exists. This is the largest single block of held domains and the cheapest
to get wrong, because an industry page that is a line page with the trade name swapped in adds
nothing and reads as filler. Worth doing only where the trade genuinely changes the exposures —
roofing and trucking do, a country club mostly does not.

**`bestemployerpayroll.com`** is not an insurance line and should be retired or repurposed rather
than given a page.

### Why this is a program rather than a pass

Each line page carries roughly 40 sourced statements across the dimensions, and each statement
needs a primary source located, fetched, and quoted precisely. The six published pages rest on
251 source records and 1,500 recorded claims. Eight more line pages at that standard is real
research, and the review burden grows with it: every page added is another page the licensed
reviewer has to clear, on top of the 35 pages and 231 rules already waiting.

The structure is now the part that is finished. `exposures` and `claimMitigation` are required
with a minimum of three entries, every entry must cite a source the page declares, and the
mitigation field rejects price, saving, discount, credit, and guarantee language outright. A new
line page cannot ship thin or ship a sales claim; it fails the suite instead.

## What no module or worksheet will ever do

- Publish a verdict that a property or risk is standard market, non-admitted, FAIR Plan
  eligible, bound, insurable, or uninsurable.
- Claim that anything was submitted to, received by, or reviewed by an insurer.
- Accept a policy document, a declarations page, or any file upload. No file input exists
  anywhere on this site, asserted by the test suite.
- Require an email address, a phone number, or an account.
- Produce a premium, a quote, or a replacement-cost figure.
- State a legal conclusion about what a contract clause requires. That routes to a lawyer.
- Give a risk score. A single number summarising someone's insurance risk would be the most
  valuable-looking and least defensible thing on this site. The only number shown is
  information completeness, which measures the reader's own input.

## Build gate

A module ships when all of the following hold. The first five are enforced mechanically.

1. `status: 'live'` and a built route under `/tools/`.
2. Every rule passes `validateModule()`: real fields, sensible operators for each field kind,
   sourced, no banned phrasing.
3. Every rule is reachable, and none fires on an empty position.
4. Every cited source exists in the registry and has a built page.
5. Every free-text field carries a privacy note; no field asks for an identifier or health
   information.
6. Every named accessibility state implemented and reviewed at 1440, 1024, 768, 390, and 320.
7. Print output stands alone and carries the non-binding notice.
8. A named licensed reviewer has signed off. **No module has cleared this yet.**
