# Birch rebrand options

Decision record: 10 September 2026

The supplied `birch-final-favicon-package` is the canonical mark. It is used
unchanged; the interface does not redraw it, recolour it, or put it in a badge.
The existing Mobbin/Figma reference pass remains useful for information
hierarchy and component behavior. Generated visual concepts are references for
layout only and never replace the supplied logo asset.

## Option A — Birch estate, two clear rooms (selected)

`Birch Research` is the citable primary product at `birch.insure` and `Birch`
is the public umbrella. `commons.birch.insure` is the independent community
room. The two origins share the bird, type system, blue action language, and
navigation vocabulary, but the Commons keeps its cooler paper and has no agency
branding or evidence status colors.

This is the current implementation because it preserves the important boundary:

- Research publishes sourced answers, coverage context, figures, and tools.
- Community publishes questions, moderated case reports, and practitioner notes.
- A community post never becomes a citation or a verdict by accident.
- Licensed help remains an explicit, optional handoff rather than the default CTA.

## Option B — One Birch origin, two top-level sections

Move the evidence room to `birch.insure/research` and keep the Commons at
`birch.insure/community`. This is simpler to explain and makes the umbrella
feel like one product, but it changes every canonical URL, dataset reference,
machine-readable record, sitemap, and external citation. It should only happen
as a planned migration with redirects and a new frozen release; it is not a
search-and-replace rebrand.

## What the interface now says

The primary CTA is blue and asks a person to `Ask Birch`. Secondary CTAs let
them `Browse coverage` or read the question library. A separate community entry
point says `Share what happened` and links to the Commons. Gold remains
reserved for resolved evidence and citation markers, so the brand blue does not
pretend that a human account is a verified finding.

The finalized interface lock is intentionally spare: Newsreader for editorial
headlines, Schibsted Grotesk for interface and body copy, and IBM Plex Mono for
metadata and evidence labels. Pale paper backgrounds, thin rules, restrained
corner radii, and one blue action color keep the research surface calm. The
home page does not ask for an email or account; it sends readers directly into
the research, with Commons as the optional next step.

All research and community content should be finalized before the licensed
review pass. The reviewer should receive one stable content snapshot, including
the final copy, source mappings, contribution labels, and boundary language,
rather than reviewing a moving target.

## Landing directions for aesthetic review

Three planning renderings were generated for this pass. They share the final
bird asset, the pale-paper Birch palette, and the Newsreader / Schibsted
Grotesk / IBM Plex Mono type system; they differ only in the first-viewport
hierarchy.

- **Editorial research-first** — a large research promise, one focused question
  field, a quiet source-proof row, and a compact “How Birch works” rail. This is
  the current implementation and the recommended starting point.
- **Guided question-first** — a centered launcher with starter paths for
  Homeowners, Auto, and Business. It makes the product feel more like a utility
  and is the strongest option if question discovery is the primary behavior.
- **Two rooms** — a shared question field followed by equal Research and
  Commons entry panels. It explains the Birch umbrella most clearly, but its
  imagery is more expressive than the research surface needs to be.

The current implementation lock and full feature sequence are in
`BIRCH-EIGHT-FIGURE-PRODUCT-PLAN.md`. Product-specific forums, threads, and
experience pages remain a separate build behind the Commons readiness gate. The
generated images are review references, not production logo or illustration
assets.
